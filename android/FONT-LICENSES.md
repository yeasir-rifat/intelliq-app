Times New Roman, Georgia and Cambria are proprietary Microsoft fonts. Redistributing them inside a published app
needs a licence that allows it, so check this before releasing the app publicly. Times New Roman and Cambria were
supplied as Regular only, so their bold and italic are synthesised by the text engine.
